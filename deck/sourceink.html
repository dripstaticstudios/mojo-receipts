Dripstatic Studios · Drawing

SourceInk

A hand-copy drawing reference companion. You lay a reference beside your drawing and copy it by hand — SourceInk is the companion surface that holds the reference while your hand does the learning.

The companion, not the shortcut

Reference beside the work

Hand-copying is a deliberate way to learn: the reference sits beside your drawing, and you copy it by hand. SourceInk exists to be that beside-the-drawing surface.

Your hand does the drawing

SourceInk is a companion for the practice — it serves the copying, it doesn't do the copying for you.

Pen input is first-class

Built-in pen support is a market gap most drawing apps ignore. SourceInk's stance is the opposite: pen input is treated as first-class — the pen is the primary way you work, not an afterthought bolted onto a touch interface.

Direction · Exploration — not a shipped feature

The Mojo angle: ink at kernel speed

Direction, clearly labeled: the Mojo Edge Program proved — with receipts — that a compiled Mojo engine can search vectors fast, and repair a search index instead of rescanning it. The exploration for SourceInk is whether that same engine room applies to ink: ink stroke processing, and searching thousands of your own strokes and pages the way the arena searched vectors.

That is an exploration direction, not a shipped SourceInk feature, and this page makes no performance claim for it. The measured numbers live in the deck, each with its receipt — nothing here borrows them.
